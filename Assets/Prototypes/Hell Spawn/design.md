## Game Feel
- Coyote timing
- Animation / attack cancels
- Subtle snap and magnetism / lock on for aerial attacks, dash and momentum attacks, and aim
- White flashes on damage
- Frame freezes for attacks (heavy especially)
- Screen shake on impacts
- Particle effects for hits
- Sound cues
- Knockback, especially on heavy attacks and high impact shots (like shotgun)

## Push / Pull | Melee vs Gun
- Melee charges special gun attack
- Gun charges special melee attack
- Enemies could drop half ammo, so melee refills ammo without draining it
- End a melee combo with a gun shot finisher that refills the ammo / reloads
